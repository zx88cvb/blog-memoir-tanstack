## Design Context

### Users
Developers evaluating or adapting this TanStack template. The homepage should explain existing capabilities and demonstrate reusable component composition.

### Brand Personality
Clear, modern, approachable. Build confidence with direct descriptions of working features and useful navigation.

### Aesthetic Direction
Simple geometric composition with a friendly rounded building-block logo, deep teal (#0F766E), light teal (#99F6E4), and a small amber (#FBBF24) accent, generous spacing, and the existing typography. Warm white (#FAFAF7) in light mode, deep gray (#111816) in dark mode, and bright teal (#2DD4BF) for dark-mode links and emphasis. Use clean solid backgrounds without decorative glows. Support light and dark themes and English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese. Avoid glossy effects, gratuitous animation, and generic product claims.

### Design Principles
- Keep page composition separate from localized feature blocks and reusable presentation components.
- Use one clear primary heading and functional calls to action.
- Express composability through layered geometry rather than decorative dashboards.
- Preserve readable layouts, keyboard navigation, and visible focus on every screen size.
- Keep assets recognizable at small sizes and in both themes.
