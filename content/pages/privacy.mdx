## About this page

This page is a starting point for a privacy policy for a website built with TanStack Template. The website operator must replace or adapt it to describe the deployed application's actual data practices and contact details.

## Browser preferences

The template uses browser storage for theme preferences and a cookie for language preferences. Describe any additional cookies or storage introduced by your application.

## Information and integrations

Information processing depends on the features, hosting, and integrations enabled by the operator. If you collect user input, retain technical logs, or enable analytics, explain what information is processed, why, who receives it, and how long it is retained.

## Choices and contact

Visitors can manage cookies and browser storage through their browser settings. The operator should describe applicable privacy choices and provide a way to ask questions or submit requests.

## Updates

Review this page whenever data practices change and update the date above. See the [Terms of Service](/terms) for the companion template page.
