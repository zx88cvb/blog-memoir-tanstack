## What a generated result means

A result is a sample, not a verified identity, a postal address verification, or permission to use an address. Names, street details, cities, postal codes, phone numbers, and email addresses may be assembled for illustration. They may be incomplete, inconsistent with one another, or unsuitable for the system you are testing. A result may also accidentally match a real person, telephone number, or location.

We do not guarantee that an address exists, accepts deliveries, or belongs to nobody. Nor do we guarantee that a telephone number works, an email address can receive messages, or a city and postal code always match. Do not call, message, or send anything to generated contact details. Use controlled test destinations when your test could trigger an actual message or delivery.

Check that sample data suits your test before relying on it. For example, a value that looks like a valid address may still fail a postal database check. AddressGen is not a substitute for verified customer information, a delivery service, an identity check, or professional advice.
